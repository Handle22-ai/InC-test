# Domain at baseline checkpoint

NGPL operational notices describe gas-flow restrictions, planned outages, force majeure, availability,
administrative changes and restriction lifts. Trading relevance depends on operational impact and
context, not keywords alone. Preserve identity, timing, segments/locations, services, restriction
values and units. Scheduled-to 55% MDQ is not the same quantity as a 55% reduction.

Fourteen supplied historical labels contain seven positives and seven negatives. A direct label map
is the current narrow oracle; it does not establish general termination, materiality or late-arrival
policy. Read context/assumptions.md before interpreting findings. No trading actions are authorized.
