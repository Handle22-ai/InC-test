I do not approve TZ-NGPL-001 as written.

Do not infer America/Chicago or any other timezone from an unzoned NGPL
timestamp without source support.

Adopt this bounded rule instead:

A source-grounded operational disruption may classify as SIGNAL_CANDIDATE
even when the source timestamp timezone is unresolved.

Unresolved source timezone:
- remains explicitly UNKNOWN;
- prevents automatic current/future actionability;
- prevents recommendation authorization;
- requires REVIEW_REQUIRED at the actionability/authorization boundary;
- must not be silently converted to UTC, Central Time, or another zone.

Where a source timestamp has an explicit trusted timezone, normal
current/future/historical evaluation may proceed.

This changes classification/actionability separation only. It does not
authorize delivery, materiality, or a recommendation.
