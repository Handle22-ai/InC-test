ANR/NGPL SOUTH JOLIET AT CAPACITY

SEGMENT 25 – STATION MAINTENANCE – CS 302

 

As posted on Natural's Outage Impact Report (X25-1527903), Natural will be performing station maintenance at Compressor Station 302 (CS 302) located in Montgomery County, Texas (Segment 25 of Natural's Texok Zone).  Natural is required to reduce the maximum operating capacity eastbound into Segment 25 from Segment 26 and Segment 22, thus limiting Natural's throughput capacity eastbound through CS 302 during this restriction.

 

The scheduling constraint will be at CS 302; therefore, any gas received west of CS 302 for delivery east of CS 302 will be impacted for the duration of the restriction.  The Pooling Point - Texok Zone (LOC 10560) is located west of the constraint.  Firm transportation associated with storage withdrawals may be impacted.

 

As such, effective for gas day Wednesday, April 1, 2026, Timely Cycle, and continuing through gas day Friday, May 1, 2026, Natural will schedule Primary Firm and Secondary in-path Firm transports to no less than 55% of contract MDQ through CS 302.  AOR/ITS and Secondary out-of-path Firm transports will not be available for the duration of this restriction.

 

The stated scheduling percentage is based upon the current level of firm capacity contracted for during this restriction and is subject to change based upon operational conditions and Shipper utilization.

 

Continue to monitor Natural's interactive website for any updates.  Please contact your Account Director or Scheduling Representative if you have any questions.
