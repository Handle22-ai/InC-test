STATION 302 – REPLACE REGULATOR – UPDATE

SEGMENT 8 - COMPRESSOR STATION 168 – UNIT MAINTENANCE

 

As posted on Natural's Outage Impact Report (X26-679081), Natural will be performing unit maintenance at Compressor Station 168 (CS 168), located in Bailey County, Texas (Segment 8 of Natural's Permian Zone).  This maintenance work requires Natural to reduce the maximum operating capacity northbound into Segment 8 from Segment 9, thus limiting Natural's throughput capacity northbound through Compressor Station 167 (CS 167).

 

The scheduling constraint will be Segment 9 at CS 167; therefore, any gas received south of CS 167 for delivery north of CS 167 will be impacted for the duration of the restriction.  The Pooling Point - Permian Zone (LOC 25077) is located south of the constraint.  Additionally, firm transportation nominated from receipt points south of CS 167 (including the Permian Pool) for injection at any storage point will be impacted.

 

As such, effective for gas day Thursday, October 1, 2026, Timely Cycle, and continuing through gas day Wednesday, October 7, 2026, Natural will schedule Primary Firm and Secondary in-path Firm transports to no less than 76% of contract MDQ through CS 167.  AOR/ITS and Secondary out-of-path Firm transports will not be available for the duration of this restriction.

 

The stated scheduling percentage is based upon the current level of firm capacity contracted for during this restriction and is subject to change based upon operational conditions and Shipper utilization.

 

Continue to monitor Natural's interactive website for any updates during this restriction.  Please contact your Account Director or Scheduling Representative if you have any questions.
