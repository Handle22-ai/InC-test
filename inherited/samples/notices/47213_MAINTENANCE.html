EXPECTED CAPACITY FOR JANUARY 2002

SEGMENT 15 – STATION MAINTENANCE - COMPRESSOR STATION 801 – UPDATE 2

 

The scheduling dates have changed, as noted below. See previous Notice ID 47157 dated August 13, 2026.

 

As posted on Natural's Outage Impact Report (X26-702063), Natural will be performing station maintenance at Compressor Station 801 (CS 801) located in Carter County, Oklahoma (Segment 15 of Natural's Texok Zone).  Natural is required to reduce the maximum operating capacity eastbound into Segment 15 from Segment 2, thus limiting Natural's throughput capacity eastbound through CS 801 during this outage. 

 

The scheduling constraint will be at CS 801; therefore, gas received west of CS 801 for delivery east of CS 801 may be impacted. The Midcontinent Pool (PIN 25078) is located west (upstream) of the constraint. 

 

As such, effective for gas day Monday, September 21, 2026 Timely Cycle and continuing through gas day Friday, October 23, 2026 (previously Wednesday, September 16, 2026, Timely Cycle, and continuing through gas day Tuesday, October 13, 2026), Natural will schedule Primary Firm and Secondary in-path Firm transports only through CS 801.  AOR/ITS and Secondary out-of-path Firm transports will not be available for the duration of this restriction.

 

Continue to monitor Natural's interactive website for any updates to the end date during this restriction.  Please contact your Account Director or Scheduling Representative if you have any questions.
